# Tlahtoa: Indigenous Women Empowerment Platform

## Overview

**Tlahtoa** (meaning "to speak" in Nahuatl) is an inclusive web platform created to support Indigenous women facing gender-based violence. Inspired by recent events in my community, where young Indigenous women have been repeatedly silenced, **Tlahtoa** aims to be a safe space where women can report abuse anonymously, access essential resources, and connect with their cultural heritage. The platform primarily serves Indigenous communities, providing multilingual support in Nahuatl, Spanish, and English, with plans to expand into other Indigenous languages like Zapotec and Mixtec.

## Objectives

- **Empower Indigenous Women**: Offer a secure space for reporting abuse and accessing support.
- **Promote Cultural Awareness**: Celebrate and share Indigenous culture through art, music, poetry, and more.
- **Provide Educational Opportunities**: Help Indigenous communities access scholarships and resources for educational growth.
  
## Features

### Anonymous Reporting
A core feature is the **anonymous reporting** tool. Women can file a report on gender-based violence without revealing their identity. Reports will be sent directly to the relevant authorities, like the police, to ensure immediate action. This feature is designed to make reporting easy, safe, and accessible for Indigenous women.

### Violence Awareness and Resources
Tlahtoa provides a variety of resources to educate and support women facing violence:
- **Violence Meter**: A tool to help users identify different forms and levels of violence.
- **Resource Directory**: A list of helplines, shelters, and organizations supporting women’s rights and human rights.
- **Survivor Stories**: Stories from individuals who have overcome violence, aimed at inspiring and supporting others.
- **Informational Articles**: Content on gender-based violence, human rights, and feminism.

### Collaboration with Tlachinollan and Local Shelters
We are partnering with **Tlachinollan**, a human rights organization in the Montaña region of Guerrero, and local shelters to make Tlahtoa a reliable and secure portal for Indigenous women across the country.

### Multilingual Support
Currently available in **Spanish**, Tlahtoa will soon support **Nahuatl** and **English**, with plans to expand to other Indigenous languages like **Zapotec** and **Mixtec**. We’re working with native speakers to ensure accurate translations and a respectful representation of each language.

### Education Hub
The Education Hub aims to provide information on educational resources and scholarships specifically available to Indigenous communities, such as:
- **Scholarships**: Information on programs like **"Líderes del Mañana"** from Tec de Monterrey, **"Yo por México"** from UDEM, which offers a 100% scholarship, and other support options from UNAM and similar institutions.
- **Free Courses**: Access to free educational resources for Indigenous communities.

### Cultural Gallery
Our cultural section will feature contributions from Indigenous artists, including:
- **Music and Poetry**: Songs and poems in Indigenous languages to promote cultural preservation.
- **Art and Crafts**: A gallery of Indigenous artwork and handicrafts.
  
This section allows users to learn about and appreciate Indigenous cultures, offering a space for Indigenous artists to share their work while maintaining copyright protection.
